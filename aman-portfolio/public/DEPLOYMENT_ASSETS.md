# Deployment asset checklist

Before launch, replace these placeholders with production assets and URLs:

- `https://REPLACE_WITH_CANONICAL_DOMAIN/` in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
- `favicon-placeholder.svg` with final favicon files as needed.
- `apple-touch-icon-placeholder.svg` with a 180 × 180 PNG for broad Apple-device compatibility.
- `og-image-placeholder.svg` with a final 1200 × 630 PNG or JPEG, then update Open Graph and Twitter image URLs in `index.html`.

The current SVG social preview is a polished visual specification and development placeholder. Many social crawlers work most reliably with PNG or JPEG image URLs.
